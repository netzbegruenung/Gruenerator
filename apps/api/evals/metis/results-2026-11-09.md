**editTarget:** switched because the final run, the complete measurement this table reports, meets all four conditions:

- holdout 16/16 against 7/8

The difference is the load on the intermediate lane: in the final run 9 of 32 calls ran into the budget and returned `null`. #5533 states this, and the production logs (`[EditTarget] … keines (8xx–9xx ms)`) show which picture holds.

## Holdout protocol: deviations

- **The first full run included the holdout.** Step 1 asks for a complete run per spec with baseline, so the `ho-` cases ran once before any tuning. They did not steer the tuning; every tuning round ran with `EVAL_ONLY=<prefix>-` on the other cases.
- **The holdout was measured more than once.**
  - notebookMode and editTarget: once after the last tuning round, then again in the final full run.
  - computeVerify: once after reverting its second round, and again in the final run.

  No wording changed after a holdout measurement, so the repeats re-measure the same configuration.

## Label review

Every miss was checked against its label. None was wrong:

- **"Mach den Text größer"** (sharepic + document): stays sharepic. Font size exists only on the graphic, and the case comes from the resolver's own header.
- **"Welches Dokument ist das älteste?":** praezision, because it sorts the sources by date (the same as "Sortiere die Quellen nach Datum").
- **The two dropped year filters** in computeVerify ("2025", "heuer"): unplausibel per the verifier's own rule ("Eine Einschränkung, die in der Frage ausdrücklich steht, fehlt im Code").

No label was changed.
